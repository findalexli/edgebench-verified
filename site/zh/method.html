Method

How we checked the claims

Original-grader evidence, bounded formal models, and version-specific repair checks

1The procedure

We ask whether honest work receives the score the task promises. Finding a defect, validating a patch, integrating a release and measuring leaderboard impact are separate conclusions. Each task records the evidence and remaining limits for its current repair candidate.

  1. Pin the original and read its contract. Compare task instructions with the evaluator from the digest-pinned public image. Record source fingerprints and the grading path.
  2. Use controls that exercise the defect. Where available, run correct, triggering and wrong submissions through the original evaluator. Component checks and source arguments are identified separately from full task runs.
  3. Check independently. Use exact arithmetic, another implementation, exhaustive search or a formal model where it answers the particular question. Agreement between AI reviewers is not independent human reproduction.
  4. Follow the final score. Distinguish measured changes, upper bounds and restricted numerical estimates. A bound need not be attainable, and a component difference can be masked by an earlier grading veto.
  5. Validate the changed version. Paired controls should show that the trigger is corrected, correct work keeps its score or has an explained change, and wrong work remains rejected. Historical results cannot accept a later revision. Full release acceptance also needs the actual task command, dependencies, work/grader images and result-collection harness to work together within budget.

Historical repeatability: the saved inventory has 206 graded comparisons covering 30 repaired tasks and the removed task's partial fix; 205 comparisons ran three times, excluding 2 setup runs. It describes the recorded versions, not Datagen v2.1 or FFmpeg v2.2. Lean Analysis used a different dependency path from its actual grading command; PFR did not demonstrate recovered credit on the five actual targets; Warehouse requires a harness wait overlay whose end-to-end integration remains pending. See each package's validation status.

2Formal evidence and its limits

Lean checks proof terms and reports their axioms. TLA+ explores all reachable states within the configured finite model. Source mappings and differential checks connect these models to implementation behavior; that connection is not automatically a machine-checked equivalence proof. Two named Lean negative controls intentionally depend on an installed placeholder and are not accepted as ordinary proofs.

The saved inventory covers 26 tasks, 25 Lean files with 286 reported theorems, and 20 runs of 3 TLA+ models. It classifies 10 repair models as proved or model-checked and 4 as partial. These are model-level classifications, not certificates for the current 31 repair packages. The saved Datagen proof uses a relaxed historical year domain; the FFmpeg proof abstracts validation and does not cover v2.2; CTA models gross exposure rather than the policy's fund NAV denominator. SMT soundness covers a restricted fragment.

Saved historical reruns are recorded in RECHECK.md. The command python3 tools/check_formal.py reruns the published sources and checks the intended invariant outcomes and requested axiom reports. A passing rerun cannot establish omitted behavior or validate a later candidate it does not model.

3Limitations

  • The 51 public tasks are in scope; 83 unreleased tasks and deliberate attacks on graders are not.
  • No AI models were rerun. Some third-party submissions were regraded, but the leaderboard submissions are unavailable. Treant conversion from an average is not exact across clipping or piecewise boundaries. Bounds exceeding a ranking gap do not prove that a reversal is attainable.
  • Portfolio's +0.3921/−0.10 envelope is a restricted sensitivity sweep, not a universal bound. Equivalence's 9.67-point bound is not demonstrated attainable. Vibrating Path's zero-hit format effect is ruled out on 37 cases and unresolved on 13.
  • CPU experiments establish resource sensitivity. A wall-clock limit alone does not promise a dedicated core or equal CPU-seconds; parallelism caps are explicit policy changes. Most paired jobs used E2B sandboxes; Lean Analysis used a local host. Speed measurements can differ elsewhere.
  • CTA's NAV correction passes paired helper controls, but native integration remains unverified. Gaps in 12 of 13 regenerated Triangulation meshes remain unresolved. Finite controls and formal models do not establish whole-task correctness.
