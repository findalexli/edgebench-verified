PFR Formalization

已修复 pfr_formalization

Five statements could never be earned because their type contained a hidden `sorry`。

摘要任务说明如何评分我们的发现文件

摘要

任务
Fill in missing proofs in a project about the PFR conjecture, a mathematics result, written in Lean, a language for machine-checked math proofs.
问题所在
Five of 423 scored statements carried a real `sorry` in their type. Lean adds it without any error because of how an automatic argument was declared. The grader counts it, so no proof could earn them, and a later proof that used one of them also lost its point. Two honest proofs of one theorem in different styles earned the same points.
影响有多大
Five targets cannot be earned (1.18 points); up to nine are affected when downstream proofs use them (2.13 points, inferred). The resulting 98.82 ceiling is an upper bound, not an attained score. Recovery on the actual targets and leaderboard impact have not been measured.
我们的处理
Repair candidate: automatic arguments move into the five statements, removing sorry from their types. Analogous lemma controls pass; no proof of the five actual targets was run and the solver image was not built.

我们的发现

完整案例：测试提交、独立检验和修复。文件路径指向代码仓库中的位置；链接在 GitHub 上打开。案例译自英文原文；如有出入，以英文原文为准。

Outcome: Type-contamination repair validated; recovered target credit and solver-image integration pending. Five scored statements carry a real sorry in their type, added silently by how Lean's module system handles an automatic argument declared with variable. So no submission can earn them (1.18 points out of 100), and a later proof that uses one of them is also marked as depending on sorry. The fixed version writes the argument into the five statements; nothing else changes. Honest proofs of one theorem in two styles score identically.

What the task asks

The solver works in a project on the PFR conjecture (a result in additive combinatorics) written in Lean 4, a language for machine-checked math proofs. The prompt asks the solver to "finish all sorries in this folder" (a sorry is a placeholder for a missing proof) and says "we value minor progress". It warns: "do not modify the signature of existing lemma/theorem/def as these changes will not pass the judge evaluation", and "we check the axioms transitively, finishing a theorem without completeing precedent lemma will not count." The score is the unweighted share of baseline declarations that depend on sorry and that the submission turns into declarations using no extra axioms, with unchanged printed types. There are 423 such targets. Category: Formal Math & Theorem Proving.

What goes wrong

We checked two possible problems. The first, about targets generated by the compiler, was not observed for the one helper and two proof styles tested. The second is real: five targets cannot be earned.

One generated helper kept the same credit in two tested proof styles. This does not establish fairness for all seven generated targets or every proof style. The same grader in lean_analysis_proofs credits helper targets that the compiler generates (X._proof_N, X._simp_N, …) only when the exact generated name and printed type match. There, at least 44 helper targets (49 of 2901 targets in total, 1.69%, counting also 3 placeholder and 2 false statements) are out of reach for any honest, complete solution, and scores depend on proof style. In this task (evidence/reviews/lean_family_aux_census.md; the count of types with a real sorry comes from the type probe described below):

The helper targets are 7 _simp lemmas, and none holds its own sorry. A test with honest proofs of one theorem on the original grader:

The tested _simp helper is credited identically in these two proof styles. The other six _simp helpers were not scored.

Five targets cannot be earned. Five targets (PFR_projection, PFR_projection', torsion_free_doubling, torsion_dist_shrinking, app_ent_PFR) print μ : autoParam (Measure Ω) sorry. The binder comes from variable (μ : Measure Ω := by volume_tac) in PFR/WeakPFR.lean, which is a Lean module file. A public statement there cannot refer to the private helper that holds the tactic, so Lean puts an error sorry in its place. It reports no error.

We checked inside the grader image. The type of all five contains the constant sorryAx (evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-autoparam-probe/result/out/probe_baseline.out). The grader collects axioms from a declaration's type as well as its proof (collectAxiomDeps in ListDeclAxiom.lean), and requires the type text to stay the same. So no proof can earn them.

We confirmed this with the task's own grader. A lemma proved by rfl under the same binder was marked as depending on sorry (evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-autoparam-eval/; variant T in repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/results.md). So was a lemma with ordinary arguments whose proof only applies that lemma (variant D): the grader also collects the axioms of the type of every lemma a proof uses.

In the upstream PFR proofs (teorth/pfr master), weak_PFR_asymm_prelim, weak_PFR_asymm, weak_PFR and weak_PFR_int are proved through these five, so a solver who follows them loses those four too. This follows from the measured rule and the upstream proof structure; it was not run with real proofs.

How much it matters

No submission can earn these 5 of 423 targets (5/423 = 1.18 points out of 100, measured); the score is therefore at most 418/423 = 98.82. This is an upper bound, not a demonstrated reachable score; the other 418 targets were not all proved. A solver who proves weak_PFR_asymm_prelim, weak_PFR_asymm, weak_PFR and weak_PFR_int through these lemmas loses those too: up to 9 targets, 9/423 = 2.13 points (inferred, as above). The five cost only solvers who would have proved them (the others score 0 on them either way), so rankings could change; whether any submission proved them is unknown. Re-scoring existing submissions with the fixed grader gives the same scores, because each submission carries its own copy of the old file. Only new runs, which start from the fixed file, can gain these points.

The fix

In PFR/WeakPFR.lean, the two automatic measure arguments and their probability-measure assumptions move from variable into the signatures of the five lemmas. The change is made in the grader's baseline and in the solver's starter, and the grader's build files are regenerated with the task's own tooling. The same 423 targets are scored. The five printed types change only in the automatic argument: sorry becomes a named helper such as PFR_projection._auto_1 (repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/out/listing_compare.txt). The grader, eval_cmd and the score scale (rescale: linear, 0.0 to 1.0) are unchanged. Package: repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/.

Every score in this table is 0.0, because no submission proves one of the five real targets; the status of the added lemmas is what differs. In every run the other 418 targets kept the same status, type_changed and disappeared were 0, the HASH checks on weak_PFR and entropic_PFR_conjecture passed, and eval_cmd exited 0 (repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/results.md, from the reports in evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-v2-paired-v1/ and -v2/). A type probe in the fixed baseline finds no sorryAx in the type of any of the five; their only sorryAx now comes from their own sorry proofs (repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/out/probe_v2_baseline.out).

Scores are comparable with the original. The denominator stays at 423, and a submission that does not prove one of the five, or a lemma that uses them, scores exactly as before.

How we checked it

  • Original grader, from the public image pinned by digest (toolchain v4.29.0). The task's judge.eval_cmd ran in the grader image, one container at a time with --network none. It scored the unmodified baseline sources and two honest variants of PFR/HundredPercent.lean that prove @[simp] mem_symmGroup with Iff.rfl (A) and with constructor <;> intro h <;> exact h (B). The ListDeclAxiom.lean actually used also checks HASH lines for weak_PFR and entropic_PFR_conjecture, which were true on the baseline.
  • Count inside the image. The baseline ListDeclAxiom listing was built. Every target was classified with the same target rule and the same helper-name pattern used for lean_analysis_proofs. A probe inside the image checked whether each helper target holds a sorry of its own.
  • Type probe. A Lean probe (EbvProbe.lean) in the grader image read the axioms of each of the five targets' types, separately from their proofs: sorryAx in all five in the original, in none in the fixed version.
  • Side-by-side runs. The original and fixed graders ran on E2B workers on the same four submissions (evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-v2-paired-v1/ and -v2/, both status: exit=0). The fixed-version job rebuilt the package from the public image with materialize.sh, including the Lean rebuild, checked that the regenerated listing matches the expected one byte for byte, ran docker build, and graded in the image it built.

Limits

  • No AI model or solver was run. Effects are shown only on the starter and the test submissions we wrote.
  • No real proof of the five targets was run. Their reachability in the fixed version is shown with lemmas stated the same way and with a check of their types.
  • That the four weak_PFR* targets are lost follows from the measured rule and the upstream proof structure. It was not run with real proofs.
  • The grader runs that confirmed the defect and tested the fix replaced the eval_cmd's copy of the 7.0 GB .lake with a copy of .lake/build plus a symlink to .lake/packages, for lack of disk. Every other step was verbatim, and the starter scored as in the verbatim run (0/423).
  • The fixed work image (Dockerfile.work) was not built. In the pinned work image, every PFR/*.lean file is byte-identical to the grader's (job pfr_formalization-v2-extract).
  • The test with honest proofs of one theorem covers one module.
  • A solver that edits the bodies of definitions or instances (with signatures unchanged) could still renumber helpers that depend on them. That was not measured.
  • How much the AddCombi dependency overlaps the PFR content was not checked.
  • Whether each individual target is true was not checked.
  • Whether the work image exposes the upstream proofs was not checked.
