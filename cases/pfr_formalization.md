# PFR Formalization (`pfr_formalization`)

**Outcome:** Type-contamination repair validated; recovered target credit and solver-image integration pending. <mark>Five scored statements carry a real `sorry` in their type, added silently by how Lean's module system handles an automatic argument declared with `variable`. So no submission can earn them (1.18 points out of 100), and a later proof that uses one of them is also marked as depending on `sorry`. The fixed version writes the argument into the five statements; nothing else changes. Correct proofs of one helper-bearing theorem in two styles score identically.</mark>

## What the task asks

The solver works in a project on the PFR conjecture (a result in additive combinatorics) written in Lean 4, a language for machine-checked math proofs. The prompt asks the solver to "finish **all** sorries in this folder" (a `sorry` is a placeholder for a missing proof) and says "we value minor progress". It also warns "do not modify the signature of existing lemma/theorem/def as these changes will not pass the judge evaluation", and "we check the axioms transitively, finishing a theorem without completeing precedent lemma will not count." The score is the unweighted share of baseline declarations that depend on `sorry` and that the submission turns into declarations using no extra axioms, with unchanged printed types. There are 423 such targets. Category: Formal Math & Theorem Proving.

## What goes wrong

We checked two possible problems. We did not see the first, about targets generated by the compiler, for the one helper and two proof styles we tested. The second is real, and five targets cannot be earned.

One generated helper kept the same credit in two tested proof styles. That does not establish fairness for all seven generated targets or every proof style. The same grader in `lean_analysis_proofs` credits helper targets that the compiler generates (`X._proof_N`, `X._simp_N`, …) only when the exact generated name and printed type match. There, at least 44 helper targets (49 of 2901 targets in total, 1.69%, counting also 3 placeholder and 2 false statements) are out of reach for any complete, correct solution, and scores depend on proof style. The counts for this task are below (`evidence/reviews/lean_family_aux_census.md`; the count of types with a real `sorry` comes from the type probe described later).

| Targets | Compiler-generated targets | Their share of the score | Observed fragile generated targets | `sorry` in type | P/¬P pairs | Lower bound on targets out of reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 423 | 7 | 1.65% | **0** | **5** | 0 | **5** |

The helper targets are 7 `_simp` lemmas, and none holds its own `sorry`. We tested correct proofs of one theorem on the original grader.

| Submission | Result on the original grader |
|---|---|
| unmodified starter (correct test submission) | 0/423, no `type_changed`, no `disappeared` |
| style A (`Iff.rfl`) | 2/423: `mem_symmGroup` and `mem_symmGroup._simp_1` |
| style B (tactic) | 2/423: `mem_symmGroup` and `mem_symmGroup._simp_1` |

The grader credits the tested `_simp` helper identically in these two proof styles. We did not score the other six `_simp` helpers.

Five targets cannot be earned. These targets (`PFR_projection`, `PFR_projection'`, `torsion_free_doubling`, `torsion_dist_shrinking`, `app_ent_PFR`) print `μ : autoParam (Measure Ω) sorry`. The binder comes from `variable (μ : Measure Ω := by volume_tac)` in `PFR/WeakPFR.lean`, which is a Lean `module` file. A public statement there cannot refer to the private helper that holds the tactic, so Lean puts an error `sorry` in its place without reporting an error.

We checked inside the grader image. The type of all five contains the constant `sorryAx` (`evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-autoparam-probe/result/out/probe_baseline.out`). The grader collects axioms from a declaration's type as well as its proof (`collectAxiomDeps` in `ListDeclAxiom.lean`), and requires the type text to stay the same, so no proof can earn them.

We confirmed this with the task's own grader. It marked a lemma proved by `rfl` under the same binder as depending on `sorry` (`evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-autoparam-eval/`; variant T in `repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/results.md`). It did the same for a lemma with ordinary arguments whose proof only applies that lemma (variant D), because the grader also collects the axioms of the type of every lemma a proof uses.

In the upstream PFR proofs (teorth/pfr `master`), `weak_PFR_asymm_prelim`, `weak_PFR_asymm`, `weak_PFR` and `weak_PFR_int` are proved through these five, so a solver who follows them loses those four too. This follows from the measured rule and the upstream proof structure; we did not run it with real proofs.

## How much it matters

No submission can earn these 5 of 423 targets (5/423 = 1.18 points out of 100, measured); the score is therefore at most 418/423 = 98.82. This is an upper bound, not a demonstrated reachable score, since not all of the other 418 targets have been proved. A solver who proves `weak_PFR_asymm_prelim`, `weak_PFR_asymm`, `weak_PFR` and `weak_PFR_int` through these lemmas loses those too, up to 9 targets or 9/423 = 2.13 points (inferred, as above). The five cost only solvers who would have proved them (the others score 0 on them either way), so rankings could change; whether any submission proved them is unknown. Re-scoring existing submissions with the fixed grader gives the same scores, because each submission carries its own copy of the old file. Only new runs, which start from the fixed file, can gain these points.

## The fix

In `PFR/WeakPFR.lean`, the two automatic measure arguments and their probability-measure assumptions move from `variable` into the signatures of the five lemmas. The change applies to the grader's baseline and to the solver's starter, and we regenerated the grader's build files with the task's own tooling. The grader scores the same 423 targets. The five printed types change only in the automatic argument, where `sorry` becomes a named helper such as `PFR_projection._auto_1` (`repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/out/listing_compare.txt`). The grader, `eval_cmd` and the score scale (`rescale`: linear, 0.0 to 1.0) are unchanged. Package: `repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/`.

| Submission | Original grader | Fixed version |
|---|---|---|
| unchanged starter | 0/423 | 0/423 |
| two lemmas stated like the five targets, proved by `rfl` | both marked as depending on `sorry` | both credited |
| a lemma with ordinary arguments that applies one of them | marked as depending on `sorry` | credited |
| a target proved through a lemma that still uses `sorry`, plus a lemma proved by `sorry` | rejected | rejected |

Every score in this table is 0.0, because no submission proves one of the five real targets; the status of the added lemmas is what differs. In every run the other 418 targets kept the same status, `type_changed` and `disappeared` were 0, the HASH checks on `weak_PFR` and `entropic_PFR_conjecture` passed, and `eval_cmd` exited 0 (`repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/results.md`, from the reports in `evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-v2-paired-v1/` and `-v2/`). A type probe in the fixed baseline finds no `sorryAx` in the type of any of the five; their only `sorryAx` now comes from their own `sorry` proofs (`repairs/pfr_formalization-v2/paired/out/probe_v2_baseline.out`).

Scores are comparable with the original. The denominator stays at 423, and a submission that does not prove one of the five, or a lemma that uses them, scores exactly as before.

## How we checked it

- We ran the original grader from the public image pinned by digest (toolchain v4.29.0). The task's `judge.eval_cmd` ran in the grader image, one container at a time with `--network none`. It scored the unmodified baseline sources and two correct variants of `PFR/HundredPercent.lean` that prove `@[simp] mem_symmGroup` with `Iff.rfl` (A) and with `constructor <;> intro h <;> exact h` (B). The `ListDeclAxiom.lean` actually used also checks HASH lines for `weak_PFR` and `entropic_PFR_conjecture`, which were true on the baseline.
- For the count inside the image, we built the baseline `ListDeclAxiom` listing and classified every target with the same target rule and the same helper-name pattern we used for `lean_analysis_proofs`. A probe inside the image checked whether each helper target holds a `sorry` of its own.
- A Lean type probe (`EbvProbe.lean`) in the grader image read the axioms of each of the five targets' types, separately from their proofs. It found `sorryAx` in all five in the original and in none in the fixed version.
- For the side-by-side runs, the original and fixed graders ran on E2B workers on the same four submissions (`evidence/runs/pfr_formalization-v2-paired-v1/` and `-v2/`, both `status: exit=0`). The fixed-version job rebuilt the package from the public image with `materialize.sh`, including the Lean rebuild, checked that the regenerated listing matches the expected one byte for byte, ran `docker build`, and graded in the image it built.

## Limits

- We ran no AI model or solver. The effects shown come only from the starter and the test submissions we wrote.
- We ran no real proof of the five targets. We show their reachability in the fixed version with lemmas stated the same way and with a check of their types.
- That the four `weak_PFR*` targets are lost follows from the measured rule and the upstream proof structure. We did not run it with real proofs.
- The grader runs that confirmed the defect and tested the fix replaced the eval_cmd's copy of the 7.0 GB `.lake` with a copy of `.lake/build` plus a symlink to `.lake/packages`, for lack of disk. Every other step was verbatim, and the starter scored as in the verbatim run (0/423).
- We built the fixed work image (`Dockerfile.work`) once, in the clean-machine rebuild check (`evidence/runs/materialize-check-pfr`, `status: exit=0`), and holds the fixed `PFR/WeakPFR.lean` with the locked sha256; no solver ran in it. In the pinned work image, every `PFR/*.lean` file is byte-identical to the grader's (job `pfr_formalization-v2-extract`). That check needed the work-image step moved after the grader steps for disk; `materialize.sh` now does this itself.
- Credit for the five actual targets is not verified. Showing that a real proof of the five repaired targets now earns credit needs real proofs of those theorems, which take the mathematics of the PFR project; none was available, so lemmas stated the same way stand in for them. This stays a documented limitation.
- The test with correct proofs of one theorem covers one module.
- A solver that edits the bodies of definitions or instances (with signatures unchanged) could still renumber helpers that depend on them. We did not measure that.
- We did not check how much the `AddCombi` dependency overlaps the PFR content, whether each individual target is true, or whether the work image exposes the upstream proofs.
