# PFR Formalization (`pfr_formalization`)

**Outcome:** Kept, with a note. <mark>Honest proofs of one theorem in two styles score identically, and no target was found that an honest, complete solution cannot reach.</mark>

## What the task asks

The solver works in a project on the PFR conjecture (a result in additive combinatorics) written in Lean 4, a language for machine-checked math proofs. The prompt asks the solver to "finish **all** sorries in this folder" (a `sorry` is a placeholder for a missing proof) and says "we value minor progress". It warns: "do not modify the signature of existing lemma/theorem/def as these changes will not pass the judge evaluation", and "we check the axioms transitively, finishing a theorem without completeing precedent lemma will not count." The score is the unweighted share of baseline declarations that depend on `sorry` and that the submission turns into declarations using no extra axioms, with unchanged printed types. Category: Formal Math & Theorem Proving.

## What we checked

There is no problem that changes scores enough to matter. Two possible problems were checked and ruled out.

**Targets generated by the compiler are scored fairly here.** The same grader in `lean_analysis_proofs` credits helper targets that the compiler generates (`X._proof_N`, `X._simp_N`, …) only when the exact generated name and printed type match. There, at least 49/2901 (1.69%) of targets are out of reach for any honest, complete solution, and scores depend on proof style. In this task:

| Targets | Compiler-generated targets | Their share of the score | Fragile generated targets | `sorry` in type | P/¬P pairs | Lower bound on targets out of reach |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 423 | 7 | 1.65% | **0** | 0 (see below) | 0 | **0** |

The helper targets are 7 `_simp` lemmas, and none holds its own `sorry`. A test with honest proofs of one theorem:

| Submission | Result on the original grader |
|---|---|
| unmodified starter (correct test submission) | 0/423, no `type_changed`, no `disappeared` |
| style A (`Iff.rfl`) | 2/423: `mem_symmGroup` and `mem_symmGroup._simp_1` |
| style B (tactic) | 2/423: `mem_symmGroup` and `mem_symmGroup._simp_1` |

The `_simp` helper is credited, and the credit does not depend on proof style. The other six `_simp` helpers were not scored.

**Types that appear to contain `sorry` are not missing proofs.** A plain text search for `sorry` finds 5 targets: `PFR_projection`, `PFR_projection'`, `torsion_free_doubling`, `torsion_dist_shrinking` and `app_ent_PFR`. In all five, the token comes from `μ : autoParam (Measure Ω) sorry`, which is how Lean's printer shows an argument filled in automatically by a tactic. A probe inside the image confirms that none of the five is a real missing proof, so none is a target out of reach.

The note that ships with the task: "Census: honest proofs in two styles score identically (2/423); 0 unattainable; 5 autoParam types print as sorry but are not holes".

The task is not changed; the note above ships with it.

## How much it matters

No effect on scores was found. The lower bound on targets out of reach is 0 of 423.

## How we checked it

- **Original grader, from the public image pinned by digest** (toolchain v4.29.0). The task's `judge.eval_cmd` ran verbatim in the grader image, one container at a time with `--network none --memory 4g --cpus 2`. It scored the unmodified baseline sources in the grader image. It then scored two honest variants of `PFR/HundredPercent.lean` that prove `@[simp] mem_symmGroup` with `Iff.rfl` (A) and with `constructor <;> intro h <;> exact h` (B). The `ListDeclAxiom.lean` actually used also checks HASH lines for `weak_PFR` and `entropic_PFR_conjecture`, which were true on the baseline.
- **Count inside the image.** The baseline `ListDeclAxiom` listing was built. Every target was classified with the same target rule and the same helper-name pattern used for `lean_analysis_proofs`. A probe inside the image checked whether each helper target holds a `sorry` of its own, and whether a printed `sorry` in a type is a real missing proof.

## Limits

- No AI model or solver was run. Effects are shown only on the starter and the honest variants we wrote.
- The test with honest proofs covers one theorem in one module.
- Submissions were built from the baseline sources in the grader image. It was not checked that the starter in the work image is identical to them.
- A solver that edits the bodies of definitions or instances (with signatures unchanged) could still renumber helpers that depend on them. That was not measured.
- How much the `AddCombi` dependency overlaps the PFR content was not checked.
- Whether each individual target is true was not checked.
- Whether the work image exposes the upstream proofs was not checked.
