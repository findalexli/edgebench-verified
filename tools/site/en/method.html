Method

How we checked the claims

Original-grader evidence, bounded formal models, and version-specific repair checks

1The procedure

We ask whether good-faith work receives the score the task promises. Finding a defect, validating a patch, integrating a release and measuring leaderboard impact are separate conclusions. Each task's record gives the evidence and the remaining limits for its current repair candidate.

{{fig:evidence-layers}}
  1. Pin the original and read its contract. Compare task instructions with the evaluator from the digest-pinned public image. Record source fingerprints and the grading path.
  2. Use controls that exercise the defect. Where available, run correct, triggering and wrong submissions through the original evaluator. We label component checks and source arguments separately from full task runs.
  3. Check independently. Use exact arithmetic, another implementation, exhaustive search or a formal model where it answers the particular question. Agreement between AI reviewers is not independent human reproduction.
  4. Follow the final score. Distinguish measured changes, upper bounds and restricted numerical estimates. A bound need not be attainable, and a component difference can be masked by an earlier grading veto.
  5. Validate the changed version. Paired controls should show that the trigger is corrected, correct work keeps its score or has an explained change, and wrong work remains rejected. A later revision needs its own results; historical ones do not cover it. Full release acceptance also needs the actual task command, dependencies, work/grader images and result-collection harness to work together within budget.

For historical repeatability, the saved inventory has {{n_cmp}} graded comparisons covering {{n_cmp_tasks}} repaired tasks and the removed task's partial fix; {{n_cmp3}} comparisons ran three times, excluding {{n_cmp_setup}} setup runs. 2Formal evidence and its limits

Lean checks proof terms and reports their axioms. TLA+ explores all reachable states within the configured finite model. Source mappings and differential checks connect these models to implementation behavior; that connection is not automatically a machine-checked equivalence proof. Two named Lean negative controls depend on an installed placeholder on purpose, and we do not accept them as ordinary proofs.

The saved inventory covers {{formal_tasks}} tasks, {{lean_files}} Lean files with {{lean_thms}} reported theorems, and {{tla_runs}} runs of {{tla_models}} TLA+ models. It classifies {{fix_proved}} repair models as proved or model-checked ({{fix_earlier}} of them model an earlier version of the fix) and {{fix_partly}} as partial. These classifications apply to the models; they do not certify the current {{repair-v2}} repair packages. The saved Datagen proof uses a relaxed historical year domain; the FFmpeg proof abstracts validation and does not cover v2.2; CTA models gross exposure rather than the policy's fund NAV denominator; the Graph Node proof models the first fix (v2), not v2.1's str() key handling or duplicate-key rejection; the College English model is of v2.1, not v2.2's block selection and decimal-string reading. SMT soundness covers a restricted fragment.

RECHECK.md records the saved historical reruns. The command python3 tools/check_formal.py reruns the published sources and checks the intended invariant outcomes and requested axiom reports. A passing rerun cannot establish omitted behavior or validate a later candidate it does not model.

3Limitations

  • The 51 public tasks are in scope; 83 unreleased tasks and deliberate attacks on graders are not.
  • We reran no AI models. We regraded some third-party submissions, but the leaderboard submissions are unavailable. Treant conversion from an average is not exact across clipping or piecewise boundaries. Bounds exceeding a ranking gap do not prove that a reversal is attainable.
  • Portfolio's +0.3921/−0.10 envelope is a restricted sensitivity sweep, not a universal bound. Equivalence's 9.67-point bound is not demonstrated attainable. Vibrating Path's zero-hit format effect is ruled out on 37 cases and unresolved on 13.
  • CPU experiments establish resource sensitivity. A wall-clock limit alone does not promise a dedicated core or equal CPU-seconds; parallelism caps are explicit policy changes. Most paired jobs used E2B sandboxes; Lean Analysis used a local host. Speed measurements can differ elsewhere.
  • We rebuilt 31 of the 32 packages, as committed at 83181b7f, from scratch on a clean machine. We did not rebuild Lean Analysis, because its grader image needs about 45 GB of disk. For packages revised later, only their later runs apply.
  • CTA's NAV correction passes paired native runs through the original scoring command; we graded no solver output. Triangulation's regenerated v2.2 inputs cover their convex hull exactly. Finite controls and formal models do not establish whole-task correctness.
