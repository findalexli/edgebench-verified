# EdgeBench evidence audit

## Recover, then choose the question

Resolve current authority/state from the designated coordinator. Repository copies use `docs/AUDIT-WORKFLOW.md` to locate it; local workspace/archive links are provenance, not required Git contents. Missing historical links do not authorize stale state or make another preparation/review necessary.

Read that coordinator's current `AGENTS.md`, `VERIFICATION.md`, root handoff (locally `edgebenchverified/audit/evidence/sol-cloud-authorization-v1/current-root-handoff.json`), dated brief ([local link](../../../edgebenchverified/audit/AUDIT-BRIEF.md)) and later steering. Keep one current task row: exact task/version, claim, accepted evidence/terminal handle, counterevidence, disposition, remaining gates and next decision-changing experiment. Recover the named accepted leaf and exact saved attempt **before** assigning preparation or review. Reuse unchanged sources, controls and reviews; completed science needs no rerun because collection, acceptance or root glue failed.

Honor the user deadline, host/staging guards and automatic-review holds; never retry or reroute held work. Unrelated model/formal/runner failures must not serialize unheld tasks. Current authorization permits up to 50 useful cloud workers and useful reviewed resource increases, without the removed $15 ceiling/$5 reserve. New reviewers/E2B model runs use `gpt-6.1-sol`/`xhigh`; frozen attempts retain their regime. Resets, paid fallback, publication and third-party contact need applicable authorization. Private auditor access is not untouched solver holdout or certification.

## Test the actual promise

Pin task definition, work/judge image and effective version. Read the original loader, command, evaluator and score contract before inventing schema/domain predicates. Inspect provenance/data/label/split/randomness/feedback/adaptation/rights facts needed for the question, retaining domain/expert gaps. Filtered absence is not whole-image absence. Failed authored expectations or fixture/harness failures are counterevidence to those expectations, not upstream defects.

Choose the smallest falsifiable experiment with actual inputs and the **original task path**. A census, mock or launcher success does not pilot an evaluator/compiler workload. Use relevant valid/invalid/empty/partial/malformed/timeout controls, independent small oracles or metamorphic properties; this is not a mandatory checklist for every question. Ordinary reversible bounded diagnostics need no invented source-approval prerequisite; fragile frozen workers keep their actual root/grant and intake gates.

Freeze hypothesis, exact sources/inputs, envelope, observables and necessary changed-path checks together. Reuse maintained execution/collection and accepted unchanged controls. Review the changed question, run a useful pilot, then dispatch disjoint work where supported. After one evidence-based setup correction, reassess at roughly 15 minutes and name the blocker instead of building another controller/review framework. Independent data/native/source/review lanes can proceed within shared ownership and staging bounds.

Preserve first failures and unsuccessful hypotheses. Separate readiness, original test, parser/export and cleanup failures. Collect completed slices and run only the unresolved complement; submitted jobs are not tested cases. Missing diagnostics do not establish historical cause. Private raw IDs/cells/answers/stderr and credentials stay outside public evidence; safely project connector responses and inspect source/archives before sharing.

## Repair and assess evidence

Freeze concrete native-format fixes with exact work/judge targets, patch bytes and required derived-image/manifest identities. Distinguish overlays/prototypes from integrated candidates. Match original/candidate inputs, randomness, resources and scoring, with relevant regressions and a genuinely different pass where required by the task/release protocol. Changed evaluators/types/scores/splits create distinct versions; never merge scores silently.

Label source inspection, authored controls, original-helper components, native tasks, corpus measurements, model runs and independent reproduction separately. Pin artifacts, commands, resources, outcomes and cleanup. Requested model names/messages do not prove served identity. Disclose reuse/reviewer authorship; AI agents are not three independent humans. Finite fictional controls do not establish corpus incidence or benchmark impact.

Use Lean/TLA+ for applicable mathematical/state questions without blocking unrelated tasks. Source-only proofs remain unexecuted until actual tool results; map assumptions/omissions to implementation and distinguish ordinary foundations from admitted proofs.

## Move evidence to conclusions

Decide each distinct versioned claim **confirmed, rejected or unresolved** with its contract, witness, evidence mode, required different review and material limits. Close a supportable finite source conclusion as source evidence; a separate native, rights or certification question does not keep that conclusion pending. Unknown cause/history/score impact/full-corpus coverage/optimality limits broader claims. Further work should change that decision or answer a separately named repair question.

Keep findings, repair validation and task certification separate. Exact-version task breadth is a union of IDs; deeper components add depth, not another tested task. Keep unavailable tasks/pending gates in the denominator. Preserve rejected companions and accepted exact-version exclusions; excluded versions skip inclusion gates, corrected versions need separate identity/review.

Use `VERIFICATION.md` and the mechanical checker for retain/repair/exclude/pending dispositions and benchmark release. Record named requirements as satisfied, failed, unavailable or unresolved with exact reasons; invent no sampling/domain thresholds or universal review counts. Finite controls/source GO do not clear whole inclusion gates. Preserve frozen approval bundles, certification gates and holds. Benchmark release needs its gates and applicable authorization; existing explicit instructions supply authority where applicable. Lead updates with closed evidence-backed conclusions, actual results and material limits.

## Read only the applicable lesson

Use [conditional audit lessons](references/conditional-audit-lessons.md) by trigger:

- Changed reader/consumer or combined-table/domain assumptions → **Actual inherited consumers**.
- Missing/mismatched installed files → **Installed paths and mount masking**.
- Readiness, verdict/score/config/numerical or nested-budget questions → **Evaluator diagnostics and numerical scope**.
- Proof axioms/formal mapping → **Formal assurance and correspondence**.
- Schemas/reference bases/accepted-leaf recovery/reviewer roles → **Record bindings and reviewer roles**.
- Small supplying blob or archive/member identity → **Resolve identity at the smallest artifact boundary**.

Reuse unchanged loaded guidance; E2B scheduling, credential isolation, owned cleanup and accounting follow the efficient-audits skill, not a substitute for scientific acceptance.
