# Formal results

Every task with a Lean 4 or TLA+ result. Each row links to a note that states the claim, the model and its link to the grader code, the counter-check that must fail, and the limits. Saved historical reruns are in [RECHECK.md](RECHECK.md). Recheck the published sources with `python3 tools/check_formal.py`; those records do not verify later repair candidates.
