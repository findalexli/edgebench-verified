## Reading the table

**The control holds on deterministic graders.** For the 15 tasks whose grader does not depend on time or
randomness, the v1 regrade gives the recorded score on every round but one (equivalence_class agent-8: 270 instead
of 330 passed tests under v1; v2 gives 330, the recorded value). So a v1/v2 difference on these tasks is the effect
of the repair.

**Four graders are not repeatable under v1, and the table shows by how much.**
- *grid_turing_robot:* the original grader sizes its process pool from `os.cpu_count()`, not from the container's
  CPU quota. With 4 CPUs, 23 rounds that the recorded run passed 50/50 lose one case under v1 (49/50, no valid
  score). v2 sizes the pool from the quota and gives 50/50 again. This is the defect the repair fixes. The selected
  score does not change because the best round (agent-43) passes under both.
- *integer_compression_codec:* the score has a speed term, so the v1 regrade differs from the recorded score on
  every round (by up to about 10 %). The repair also redefines the scale (the documented starter scores 0 and a
  perfect codec 100; see the package README). **v1 and v2 numbers for this task are on different scales and are
  not comparable**; 5.44 → 2.78 is a change of unit, not a change in quality.
- *graph_node_classification:* the score comes from training a model, which varies from run to run. v1 and v2
  differ from the recorded score, and from each other, by a similar amount (35.24 / 35.22 / 35.21).
- *jagua_nesting_optimization:* time-limited heuristic search, so the raw score varies by about 2.5 %.

**Where the repair changes the result:**
- *triangulation_coloring_optimization:* 58.07 → no valid score. Under the documented flip rule every scored
  submission makes a forbidden flip; the 2 × 2 test in `../qwen35-diag-triangulation/` shows that the rule, not
  the new inputs, causes this.
- *tree_block_partitioning:* 12.17 → 12.38. 29 rounds change score in both directions; the selected round moves.
- *schemathesis_config_modernization* 45.93 → 45.55 and *schemathesis_reporting_observability* 37.01 → 37.51:
  every round shifts by a small amount (config: −0.29 to −0.38 points; reporting: +0.50 to +1.33 points),
  and the selected round is the same.
- The other tasks give the same selected score under v1 and v2.
